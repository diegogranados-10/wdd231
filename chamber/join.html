Frequently asked questions

Before you join.

How long does the membership process take?

+

After receiving your application and documentation, we validate legal requirements within 3 to 5 business days. Once approved, you will receive your digital credential and access to the member portal the same day.

Can startups without a RIF join?

+

A RIF is mandatory for Silver and Gold levels. For entrepreneurs in the process of formalization, we offer free support to obtain registration, with conditional membership under the social rate.

Do NGOs pay VAT on the membership fee?

+

No. Organizations registered as non-profit are exempt under current regulations. The invoice is issued with the corresponding tax base.

Can I change levels later?

+

Yes. Level changes can be requested online through the member portal or in writing to the secretary. The new level takes effect the following monthly cycle.

Is there a discount for annual payment?

+

Yes. Prepaid annual payment has a 10% discount across all three levels. Available at the time of joining or when renewing.
