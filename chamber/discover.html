History

Three centuries in the Valley.

From a colonial parish founded by Carmelites to a logistics hub on the Caracas–Tuy corridor.

  1. 1761

    Foundation

    Don Bartolome de Blandin establishes the parish under the patronage of Santa Teresa de Jesus. The first cacao plantations settle along the banks of the Tuy River.

  2. 1864

    Municipal capital

    After the 1864 political-territorial reorganization, Santa Teresa becomes the capital of the Independencia district and trade center of the Tuy Valley.

  3. 1894

    The railroad arrives

    The Gran Ferrocarril de Venezuela connects Caracas with Santa Teresa, transforming the local economy and opening the way for the first agri-food industry.

  4. 1968

    Chamber is established

    Twenty-two local merchants found the Chamber of Commerce of Santa Teresa del Tuy on Calle Sucre. First president: Luis A. Madriz.

  5. 2002

    Tuy Highway opens

    The new Caracas–Charallave–Santa Teresa highway corridor reduces travel time to the capital and accelerates the logistics and residential development of the area.

  6. 2026

    Tuy 2030 Plan

    The Chamber coordinates with city halls and the private sector on the regional competitiveness plan for the next five years.
